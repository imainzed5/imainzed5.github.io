# Whisk Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 27, 2026**

Whisk is a local-first focus timer. This policy describes the information handled by the Whisk app.

## Information stored on your device

Whisk stores Focus session records, including task names, tags, durations, dates, and selected Focus Modes. It also stores your preferences and Miso’s focus-related progression. This information is kept in Whisk’s local app storage.

Whisk does not create user accounts. The app does not send this information to the developer, and it does not include advertising or analytics services.

## Notifications

If you enable notifications, Whisk schedules local notifications to remind you when a Focus session or break ends. While a Focus session or break is running and Whisk is in the background, Whisk also shows a silent, ongoing notification with the time remaining; it disappears when you return to the app or the session ends. These notifications are created on your device, contain only timer status, and are not sent to the developer or any server. Android controls notification sound and vibration through its notification settings.

## Backup, retention, and deletion

For V1 on Android, Whisk data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device app-data transfer. Whisk has no account or cloud recovery. To remove its local data, clear Whisk’s app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use the support email shown in the App support section of Whisk’s Google Play listing. If you contact support, the message and any details you choose to include are delivered through your email provider; Whisk does not send a support message automatically.
